AI Growth Engine — Design Document
1. What Is It?
A standalone SaaS add-on that gives e-commerce businesses AI-powered marketing — product descriptions, social media content, email campaigns, promotional images, and video reels — generated in seconds from their product data.
The key point: it is not tied to your own commerce platform. It connects to Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, or your own platform via a connector. That multiplies your market without building multiple products.

Why this wins in India specifically: most global AI marketing tools (Jasper, Copy.ai, AdCreative.ai) don't understand Hinglish content, Indian festivals (Diwali, Navratri, Eid, Pongal), WhatsApp-first marketing, or GST-aware copy. You do.

2. Architecture — Provider Abstraction
You never call OpenAI, Gemini, or any provider directly from business logic. Every capability goes through an interface. The implementation behind it can change without touching feature code.

Credit ledger design — append-only, never update rows:
sqlAiCreditLedger
  LedgerId       BIGINT PK AUTO_INCREMENT
  TenantId       CHAR(36) FK
  TransactionType  ENUM('Allocation','Debit','TopUp','Refund','Expiry')
  Credits        INT          -- positive = add, negative = deduct
  BalanceAfter   INT          -- running balance (denormalised for speed)
  ReferenceId    VARCHAR(100) -- GeneratedContentId, TopUpOrderId, etc.
  CreatedAt      DATETIME
-- Balance = latest BalanceAfter row for this TenantId
-- Credits expire at end of billing period; no rollover on base plan

7. Build Stages — AI Engine

Build AI Growth Engine after Commerce Platform V2 is stable. The own-platform connector depends on V2 tenant infrastructure.

AI-0 — Foundation
New ecomm.ai project, provider abstraction interfaces, credit system tables, first text provider (Gemini Flash), cost tracking, AI plan management in Super Admin.
AI-1 — Text Generation (Ship First, Prove the Model)
Product description generator, social captions, WhatsApp copy, email copy, hashtag generator, language selector (English / Hindi / Tamil / Telugu). This is the MVP — ship it, charge for it, validate demand before building images or video.
AI-2 — Platform Connectors (The Market Multiplier)
OwnPlatformConnector (internal), ShopifyConnector (OAuth), WooCommerceConnector (API key), CSV upload fallback. The moment Shopify merchants can use your engine, your addressable market multiplies.
AI-3 — Image Generation
Poster and banner generator, 20+ starting templates, background removal, social size presets (1:1, 9:16, 16:9).
AI-4 — Campaign Builder
Multi-channel wizard, schedule per channel, basic analytics.
AI-5 — Video Generation
Slideshow-to-video, short Reels, festival videos. Hard credit gates — maximum 50 videos/month on highest plan, no exceptions.
AI-6 — Indian Market Differentiators
Festival calendar (30+ Indian festivals, auto-suggest campaigns), Hinglish mode, regional languages (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi), WhatsApp broadcast format, GST-aware pricing copy.
