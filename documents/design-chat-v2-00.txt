It depends on how much AI work you do

The biggest factor is usage, not the number of customers.

Let's assume your AI Marketing Engine does:

Generate product descriptions
Generate Instagram captions
Generate Facebook posts
Generate hashtags
Generate email content
Generate WhatsApp campaigns
Generate blog articles
Generate SEO content

These are mostly text generation tasks, which are relatively inexpensive.

For a typical small business:

20 product descriptions/month
30 social media posts/month
10 email campaigns/month
20 WhatsApp messages/month

That's around 80 AI text generation requests/month.

Using current LLM APIs, this often costs well under ₹100 per customer per month, depending on the model and prompt sizes. If you use smaller, efficient models for routine marketing copy, it can be significantly less.

How Canva and Similar Platforms Do It

Most companies don't offer unlimited AI.

Instead they have policies like:

500 AI credits/month
1,000 AI credits/month
10 videos/month

After that:

Buy more credits.

This keeps costs under control.

Since you're a software architect...

I would never tie the AI Marketing Engine directly to a single provider like OpenAI.

Instead, design it like this:

Marketing Engine

        │

-------------------------

LLM Provider Interface

-------------------------

       │

OpenAI

Google Gemini

Anthropic Claude

Local Llama

DeepSeek

Future Models

The same idea applies to image and video generation:

Image Service

       │

------------------------

OpenAI Images

Google Imagen

Stable Diffusion

Flux

Future Providers

Then your marketing engine simply calls an interface such as:

IAITextService.GenerateMarketingContent(...)

or

IImageGenerationService.GeneratePoster(...)

The implementation decides which provider to use. That gives you flexibility to optimize for cost, quality, availability, or even customer preference without changing your application logic.

My recommendation

For your AI Marketing Engine, I'd structure pricing like this:

Base Commerce Platform: ₹499–₹999/month
AI Marketing (Text + Basic Images): ₹499–₹999/month
AI Video Generation: Pay per use or via monthly credits

The reason is simple: text AI is inexpensive enough to bundle, while video AI should almost always be metered, because a small number of heavy users can otherwise consume far more resources than your subscription revenue covers. This model keeps your margins predictable while still giving customers access to powerful AI features.
